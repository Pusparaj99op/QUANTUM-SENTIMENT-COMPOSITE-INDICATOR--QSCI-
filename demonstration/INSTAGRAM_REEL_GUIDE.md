# 🎬 QSCI Instagram Reel Script & Production Guide

## 📱 Reel Details
- **Duration:** 60-90 seconds (optimal for algorithm engagement)
- **Format:** 9:16 vertical (1080x1920)
- **Language:** English
- **Editing Level:** Mid-level (cuts, text overlays, transitions)
- **Platform:** Instagram Reels

---

## 🎥 EQUIPMENT SETUP

### Camera: Samsung S24 Ultra
**Recommended Settings:**
- Resolution: 4K @ 30fps (or 1080p @ 60fps for smoother slow-mo)
- Focus: Auto-focus with face tracking ON
- Stabilization: Super Steady ON
- Aspect Ratio: 9:16 (vertical)
- HDR: OFF (easier color grading)
- Grid: Enable rule of thirds

### Ring Light Setup
**Position:**
- Distance: 2-3 feet from your face
- Height: Eye level or slightly above
- Angle: Directly in front (creates catch lights in eyes)
- Brightness: 70-80% (not too harsh)
- Color Temperature: 5000K-5500K (daylight, looks professional)

### RGB Lights Setup
Create a modern tech/coding aesthetic:

**Option 1 - Dual Side Lighting:**
```
        [Ring Light - White/Daylight]
                    |
                    ▼
    [Purple RGB] ← [YOU] → [Cyan/Teal RGB]
        45° angle         45° angle
```
- Left RGB: Purple (#a855f7) at 30-40% brightness
- Right RGB: Teal/Cyan (#4ecdc4) at 30-40% brightness
- Creates cinematic color separation

**Option 2 - Background Accent:**
```
    [Ring Light]
         |
         ▼
       [YOU]
         |
    [RGB Strip behind - slow color fade]
```
- RGB strip placed behind you (out of frame)
- Set to slow fade between purple → blue → teal
- Creates dynamic background glow

### Camera Angle
```
                 📱 Camera (S24 Ultra)
                   |
                   | 10-15° angle (slightly above eye level)
                   |
                   ▼
                 [YOU]
               Standing/Seated
```
- Tripod height: Slightly above eye level (makes face look slimmer)
- Distance: 3-4 feet from camera
- Frame: Head + shoulders + some headroom
- Look directly into camera lens (NOT screen)

## 🎨 EDITING GUIDELINES

### Text Overlays
- **Font:** Clean sans-serif (Montserrat, Inter, or SF Pro)
- **Colors:** White text with subtle shadow OR Gold (#ffd700) for highlights
- **Animation:** Fade in from bottom, 0.3s duration
- **Position:** Lower third or centered

### Transitions
- Use simple cuts (80% of transitions)
- Zoom cuts for emphasis
- Swipe transitions for "list" sections
- NO excessive effects

### Music
- Lo-fi tech beat OR subtle electronic ambient
- Volume: 20-30% under voiceover
- Suggestion: Search "tech presentation music" or "coding background music"

### Color Grading
- Slightly desaturated base
- Lifted blacks (not pure black)
- Teal highlights in shadows
- Warm midtones
- This matches your RGB lighting setup

### Pacing
- Cuts every 2-4 seconds
- Faster cuts during "features" section
- Slower, steady shots for formula explanation

## 💡 PRO TIPS

### Delivery
1. **Energy:** Slightly higher than natural conversation
2. **Speed:** 150-170 words per minute (engaging but clear)
3. **Pauses:** Brief pauses before key terms for emphasis
4. **Eye Contact:** Look at camera lens, NOT yourself on screen

### Lighting Check
Before filming:
- [ ] No harsh shadows under eyes
- [ ] Catch lights visible in both eyes
- [ ] RGB colors visible but not overpowering
- [ ] Background not brighter than face

### Audio
- Film in a quiet room
- Use S24 Ultra's built-in mic OR external lav mic
- Test audio levels before full recording
- Clap at the start of each take (helps sync in editing)

### Background
- Clean, minimal setup
- Monitor showing code/charts visible behind you
- RGB lights creating colored edge lighting
- Remove distractions (cables, clutter)
